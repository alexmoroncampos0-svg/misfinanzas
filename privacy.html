Privacy policy

My Finances app (Mis Finanzas) · Effective September 27, 2026

This policy explains what data the My Finances app collects, what it is used for, and what you can do about it. It is written in plain language on purpose.

Who is responsible for your data

Alex Moron Campos, the app's developer, based in Bolivia. Contact: misfinanzas.bo@gmail.com

What data we collect

DataWhat for
Email addressTo create your account, verify it, and let you reset your password.
NameTo greet you inside the app.
PasswordTo protect your account. It is stored encrypted and nobody, not even the developer, can see it.
Entries you record: income, expenses, savings, tithe, with date, category, amount and notesThis is the app's content: it is used to calculate your summaries and charts.
Savings goals, loans and habits you recordTo show your progress, your streaks and due dates.
Your salary, country, currency and limits set in SettingsTo show amounts in your currency, calculate the tithe, and warn you when you go over your limit.
Your Pro subscription statusTo know whether you should see ads. See “Purchases” below.

Advertising

The free version shows a small ad at the bottom of the screen. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads.

  • Your financial data is never shared with advertisers: Google does not receive your entries, amounts, loans or habits.
  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • With a Pro subscription, no ads are shown or loaded.

Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The Pro subscription is paid through Google Play. We never see or store your card details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and activate it on your account, we use RevenueCat, which receives from Google Play the product purchased, dates and subscription status, together with your account's internal ID (not your email or name).

Reminders

If you record a loan with a due date, the app schedules notifications on your phone for the day before and the day it is due. These reminders are created and stored only on your phone; they are not sent to any server. You can turn them off in Android's notification settings.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone, under any circumstances.
  • We do not use analytics tools to track what you do inside the app.
  • We do not connect to your bank or your cards. You enter everything by hand.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Supabase, a database service hosted on servers in South America (São Paulo, Brazil). Information travels encrypted between your phone and the server.

The app enforces security rules at the database level (Row Level Security), so each user can only read and change their own data.

Your phone also keeps a local copy of your entries so the app works offline. That copy lives in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Who we share data with

Only with the providers needed for the app to work:

  • Supabase: database and account system (servers in Brazil).
  • Email provider: sends only account verification and password reset emails.
  • Google (AdMob and Google Play): ads in the free version and subscription billing.
  • RevenueCat: purchase verification.

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States or other countries, under their own safeguards. We may also disclose information if a competent authority requires it through a valid legal order.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we erase your data from our servers, with no backups kept. Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are subject to their own legal and accounting obligations.

Your rights

  • See and correct your data: inside the app, at any time.
  • Take it with you: in Settings you can export your entries and loans as a CSV file.
  • Delete your account and all your data: from Settings, inside the app, or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Withdraw your consent to personalized ads, where it was requested.
  • Ask us who we share your data with, or file a complaint with the data protection authority in your country or state.

For any of these requests, email us; we reply within 15 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children of that age.

Security

We use encrypted connections, one-way hashed passwords and rules that isolate each user's data. No system is perfect: if an incident affects your data, we will let you know by email.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything important, we will update the date above and notify you inside the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or complaints about your data: misfinanzas.bo@gmail.com
