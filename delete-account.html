Delete my account and my data

You can delete your My Finances account whenever you want, without asking anyone for permission and without giving a reason.

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Finances and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll down to My account, tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate.

By email

If you no longer have the app installed, write to misfinanzas.bo@gmail.com from the same email address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We reply within 15 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and your email address.
  • All your entries: income, expenses, savings and tithe.
  • Your savings goals, loans and habits.
  • Your settings: salary, country, currency and limits.

Nothing is kept after deletion, and it cannot be undone.

If you want to keep your history, go to Settings → Export entries and loans (CSV) before deleting and save the file on your phone.
